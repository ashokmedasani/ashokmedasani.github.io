Concrete Crack Detection — CNN & ResNet

Can a neural network spot structural cracks in concrete before an inspector does? Three architectures trained and benchmarked to find out.

  • Trained baseline CNN, BatchNorm CNN, and BatchNorm + augmentation in PyTorch
  • Benchmarked against ResNet transfer learning
  • Key finding: augmentation mattered more than depth — the augmented model generalized far better on unseen surfaces
  • Live interactive demos deployed on Hugging Face Spaces

Steel Manufacturing Client Purchase Prediction

What actually drives B2B purchasing on an order-to-purchase platform? Eight model families benchmarked to find the answer.

  • Compared OLS, Ridge, Lasso, Polynomial, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and Neural Networks on R², Adjusted R², RMSE, and MSE
  • Finding: ensemble methods outperformed linear models, indicating complex non-linear relationships in purchasing behavior
  • Built an interactive Streamlit application running the full workflow — preparation, EDA, training, and model comparison
