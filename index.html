About

I'm Ashok Medasani — a data and analytics professional with an M.S. in Analytics from Saint Louis University and 4+ years of experience across banking technology and analytics.

I started in banking infrastructure at Bank of America, supporting HP NonStop systems behind card and transaction processing. Two things pushed me toward analytics. The first was impatience with manual work: internal audit reporting was rebuilt by hand every week, so I automated it — pick a date range, get the report with the charts already built. That saved 100+ operational hours a month and led to my role as change/QA subject matter expert, which I went on to lead with a team of 15.

The second was more interesting. Our system alerts looked random, but they weren't — after a change went in, the alerts it caused often surfaced later rather than immediately. I kept thinking: this is a pattern, so why are we reacting to it instead of predicting it? I didn't have the tools then. That question is what brought me here.

Since then I've built CareFinder, a Django healthcare application forecasting hospital cost and patient visits with Random Forest and XGBoost across ten integrated datasets; trained and deployed CNNs; shipped a RAG assistant; and designed and deployed a full multi-surface platform on AWS. I'm now focused on data analytics, data science, and analytics engineering roles with a strong predictive component.

Outside of work, I enjoy playing chess, exploring new places, and spending time with friends — it keeps me grounded and thinking clearly.
